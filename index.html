Where the business is

Three windows, one method. Sales are net of discounts and comps, gift cards and deposits are excluded, and every window compares the same 78 stores with the same weeks a year earlier.

Two paths, fifty-two weeks

Rolling four-week comp for the fifteen and for the other 63 stores. The rest of the estate climbed from double-digit declines last autumn to flat by late summer; the fifteen did not.

Momentum, four weeks at a time

Comp by four-week block for each of the fifteen, the other 63 and the estate. Read left to right: the colour should be moving toward cream.

By region

Same three windows, by operating region. The last column is the fifteen's share of each region's twelve-week shortfall, with the comp of the rest of the region beside it.

Three years of monthly sales

The 74 stores trading throughout 2024–26, with Chef Hwy's Jazz Fest fortnight taken out of each year. 2025 finished about 11% below 2024 and autumn 2025 ran 13–15% below the year before, so this autumn laps a soft base. January and February 2026 were flat on a snow-hit January 2025 and an early Mardi Gras; March gave it back.

The two stores not yet in the base

Gaslamp (opened June 2025) and Downtown Fort Lauderdale (February 2025) are shown here rather than in the totals. Both are losing ground on their opening year.

Gaslamp joins the comparable base for windows starting after 22 December 2026; Downtown FLL is in it from windows starting after 22 August. Unaddressed, the two are worth about two points of comp in the first quarter of 2027.

The demand backdrop

Fewer adults drink, confidence is soft, and costs are still rising faster than our prices. None of this is unique to Fat Tuesday, which is the point: the fifteen are losing ground against peers facing the same weather.
