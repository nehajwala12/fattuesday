04

Inside the stores: what the numbers point to

The average check is flat, which could suggest there is nothing to work on there. Look underneath it, though, and three things moved in opposite directions and netted out, and two of them are already being handled well somewhere in the system.

The floater programme Las Vegas is already running

A floater is a top-shelf shot poured onto the daiquiri. The Vegas team turned it into a programme this year: floater dollars are up a third and three-quarters of daiquiris now carry one. That is the reason Vegas comps held at −1% on traffic −2%, and it is a Fat Tuesday success story rather than an outside idea. It has not yet been rolled out beyond Vegas, and Arizona and Ybor do not have floaters on the menu. The chart is floater dollars per daiquiri, this year against last, for every tourist-format store.

The plan: flat by December, positive by spring

Fourteen actions, all grounded in the data above, sized on the twelve-week base (one comp point is about $165k per twelve weeks). Gross is the full gap to last year or to the market's 75th percentile. Risk-adjusted multiplies each by how confident we are and takes a further 15% off the check levers because price, mix and attach draw on the same wallet. Treat the risk-adjusted number as our estimate, not a forecast.

The bridge from −6.1% to flat

In what order

Leaks first because they cost nothing and pay in days. Then the check, because the guest is already at the counter. Then traffic, which is slower but is what carries comps through the January lap.

Every action, sized

Annualised is gross times four, the ratio of the 2025 full year to the same twelve weeks. Margin-only items carry no comp dollars.
